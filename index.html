How Our Program Works

A Prelude to Higher Education

Our institution provides flexible virtual learning options for school students of all ages from all around the world. All of our courses are certified to surpass and go beyond Common Core standards to ensure that your child is reaching their maximum potential.

Due to our adaptable virtual class options and on-demand services, those enrolled at Academic Consulting & Education (ACE) get the opportunity to progress through courses at the pace that works best for them, while still making it challenging when necessary.
