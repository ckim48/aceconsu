KCSL 2026 Contest Information

  • All KCSL 2026 contests will be held offline at two locations: Seoul and Jeju Island.
    The Intermediate/Senior and Junior/Elementary divisions will take place on different days and at different venues.
    • Intermediate / Senior Division
      • Seoul: Providence Christian Academy (PCA) Korea
        • Address: 26, Teheran-ro 34-gil, Gangnam-gu, Seoul, Republic of Korea 06223
      • Jeju: North London Collegiate School, Jeju
        • Address: 33 Globaleduro 145 beongil, Daejeongeup Seogwiposi, Jeju, South Korea 63644
    • Junior Division / Elementary Division — This year, Jr. and Elem. divisions will be held only in Seoul.
      • Seoul: Andover Collegiate Academy (ACA), Seocho Campus
        • Address: 410 Hyoryeong-ro, Seocho-dong, Seocho-gu, Seoul, South Korea 06728

Each participant (individual, not team) must register by paying a one-time fee of 100,000KRW, which covers all four contests.
