About Academic Consulting & Education

Our Background

Founded on a strong belief in accessible education, Academic Consulting & Education (ACE) is proud to bring academia to all corners of the world through our online program. Each year our student community grows and strengthens, and our alumni graduate as passionate and curious learners.

ACE initially began with 2 physical campuses in the Bay Area of California. Ever since 2006, we have been providing students with a rich and diverse learning environment in English, Mathematics, and Computer Science. Our unparalleled teaching methods help to launch students into the successful future they have always dreamed of.

We wanted to extend our vision globally, and so we are now exclusively VIRTUAL.

Regardless of where they are located, our students receive customized, high quality education from licensed teachers who are always available through online channels. We're here to provide the flexible learning experience you need.
