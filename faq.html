Frequently Asked Questions

The Answers You Need

Each student at Academic Consulting & Education has a personal, customized path that is specifically built for their goals, skill level, and pace.

Students will be sent a class invitation link to either Zoom, Google Meet, or MS Teams before class. ACE classes can be taken by anyone who has the following:

1. Computer/Laptop
2. Reliable Internet Connection
3. Headset with microphone
4. Tablet (optional)

ACE students use Google Classroom as the main education platform to receive class materials and assignments, as well as to take virtual classes on Zoom and/or Google Meet.

ACE homework and assignments will be graded virtually using the free but powerful annotation app, Kami. (Students will need to sign up for the free account.)

ACE utilizes ICanNote to screen share lecture notes, as well as to allow students the ability to download PDF files and class recordings shared by the tutor.
