Application Form

Please follow the steps below to start the application process at ACE.

  1. Fill out the form below. (New students only. If you have previously taken classes at ACE, please contact us.)
  2. ACE staff will then send you an evaluation test and answer sheet for your child to complete and return to ACE.
  3. After an analysis of the evaluation results, ACE will contact you to make an appointment online for a virtual consultation.
  4. A counselor will discuss the best class options with you based on the results, and you may then choose to register for the classes discussed.

Important Notes

  • Enrollment in all ACE programs is on a first-come, first-served basis. We recommend enrolling early to avoid the last-minute rush and secure a place in the appropriate class. Class spots are limited.
  • Information regarding scheduling and pricing is subject to change without notice.
  • The email addresses you submit on the online registration form will be used to notify you of exam dates, special announcements, and new courses. We will never share your contact information with anyone else.
  • Please find online payment options on the contact page.
